CEA internship: more realistic reflections in virtual reality

Master's thesis internship · CEA Saclay · January to July 2025

C++CUDAOpenMPPyTorchGaussian SplattingRay TracingVR
Internship report (PDF, French, 6 MB)

The problem

Gaussian Splatting recreates a 3D scene from photos and displays it in real time, but it renders reflections poorly (shiny surfaces, glass, metal objects).

What I did

I first tested ray tracing, which simulates the path of light: accurate, but too slow for a virtual reality headset. I then added a mathematical model of reflections to the standard renderer.

The result

Much more realistic reflections, closer to the real photo on all three test scenes, at the same speed as the base method.
