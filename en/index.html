CEA internship results

The problem

Gaussian Splatting recreates a 3D scene from photos and displays it in real time, but it renders reflections poorly (shiny surfaces, glass, metal objects).

What I did

I first tested ray tracing, which simulates the path of light: accurate, but too slow for a virtual reality headset (60 frames per second minimum). I then added a mathematical model of reflections (Spherical Gaussians) to the standard renderer.

The result

Images closer to the real photo on all three test scenes, still fast enough for VR, with a 50% larger model and 15% longer training.

Drag the slider: the reference method (gsplat) on the left, my method on the right. PSNR measures similarity to the real photo (higher is closer). Details in the internship report (in French).
